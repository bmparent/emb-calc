# EmbroideryCalc iOS privacy policy

Effective September 7, 2026. Applies to the EmbroideryCalc iOS companion, version 1.0.0.

EmbroideryCalc estimates embroidery production, prepares quotes and keeps job records. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, purchases or an app-operated cloud service.

DST designs, filenames, stitch previews, photos and color samples are processed on your device. Jobs, machine profiles, prices and sampled stitch previews are saved in the app's private files. A previous snapshot is retained for recovery. Recovery operations archive unreadable data before replacing it. Custom color references use local web storage. Source photos are not retained after you leave the tool; keep your originals separately.

Camera access is optional and used to photograph artwork for color comparison. You can choose an existing image instead. You choose whether to export a PDF quote, job backup or recovery file, and choose its destination through the system share sheet. That destination controls the exported copy. EmbroideryCalc does not automatically upload your jobs or files to its developer.

Job backups include jobs and machine profiles. They exclude original DST files, photos and custom color CSVs. Keep those original files separately. Export backups before deleting the app, clearing device data or changing devices. iOS device backups, including any iCloud backup you enable, are governed by your Apple settings. The app does not provide automatic cloud synchronization.

Data remains until removed with app data or overwritten through your actions. Old ordinary snapshots and temporary share files are cleaned up as the app operates; preserved recovery archives remain in app storage. Removing the app can remove the saved data. Copies you export remain at the destinations you chose.

External references and the support tracker have their own privacy policies. Our [public support tracker](https://github.com/bmparent/emb-calc/issues) requires a GitHub account to post. Do not post confidential artwork, personal information, customer details or job backups in public issues. Report errors using the app version, device model and reproduction steps.

This is a general productivity tool, not a service directed to children. Material privacy changes will update this policy before release. Contact the [support tracker](https://github.com/bmparent/emb-calc/issues) with privacy questions; the developer cannot retrieve device-local data that you have not shared.
